Take custody of your key
custody: the platform holds an encrypted copy

Your owner key was generated on our own infrastructure and stored encrypted with AES-256-GCM. It is shown only while you hold the button, and it is never sent anywhere else.

•••• hidden — press & hold to reveal ••••
Never share this. Anyone with your private key controls your wallet and your LUV. Save it in a password manager or a hardware wallet, or import it into MetaMask. firstLUV: what to do with this key

Then finish the handoff. Once you have saved the key, tell the platform to shred its copy: the encrypted key is overwritten with random bytes seven times, blanked, the table is rewritten and a checkpoint is forced so no earlier version survives; this page shreds its own cache of the key the same way. After that the platform can deliver LUV to your address but can never sign for it. It cannot be undone. Type the last six characters of your address to confirm.
